About Me

Who is Caleb?

Caleb is a game programmer from Colorado. He has had an interest in both games and programming since he was a young child. In elementary school, he first started using Scratch and has kept coding since then. He first picked up Unity in high school and has been continuously making projects and improving.

Education

Caleb is currently a Senior at Champlain College in Vermont. He is double majoring in both Game Programming and Applied Mathematics, and has maintained a high GPA, at 3.989.
